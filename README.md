# myntra-trends-pulse-POWER-BI-dashboard 
## Project Objective
A Power BI dashboard analyzing Myntra's e-commerce fashion data across brands, pricing, discounts, and customer reviews. The dashboard provides actionable insights for pricing strategy, and brand performance evaluation through two interactive pages — Home (revenue ; discount analysis) and Trend Insights (product mix ; rating analysis).
## Dataset Used:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/skmewati/myntra-sales-dataset
## Business & Trend Insights:
- Which are the Top 10 brands by Total Revenue on Myntra?
- Which brands offer the highest average discount savings to customers?
- How much total revenue is being lost due to discount strategies (MRP vs. Selling Price gap)?
- Does offering a higher discount improve a brand's average customer rating, or is quality independent of pricing?
- Which price segment — Premium, Mid-Range, or Budget — contributes the most to Total Revenue?
## Process:
- Data Collection — Downloaded Myntra dataset from Kaggle with brand, price, MRP, ratings, and discount details.
- Data Cleaning — Removed nulls, fixed categories, and created new columns like Discount Saving and Revenue Lost.
- Dashboard Building — Built a 2-page Power BI dashboard with charts, KPI cards, and filters.
- Insights — Found that ₹82.66M was lost to discounts and customer ratings don't improve with higher discounts.
 ## Pages
- Home : Revenue, discount, and price segment analysis.
- Trend Insights : Rating trends, product mix, brand ranking.

## Key Insights:
- Top Revenue Brands - Flying Machine & WROGN generate the highest revenue.
- Discount Strategy - Mid-Range products are most popular; higher discounts = more engagement.
- Revenue Loss - ₹82.66M lost to discounts out of ₹165.76M MRP — ~50% discount leakage.
- Ratings vs. Discount - Avg rating stays ~4.0 across ALL discount levels — discounts don't improve satisfaction.
- Price MixMid - Range dominates (49.11%), Budget close behind (46.28%), Premium minimal (4.6%).
- Reviews - Roadster leads with 1,100 reviews; WROGN ranks #1 by Index score.
## Conclusion:
Myntra is a mass-market platform where 95% of products are Budget and Mid-Range, leaving Premium as an untapped opportunity.
Despite losing ₹82.66M to discounts, customer ratings stay flat at 4.0 — proving that heavy discounting does not improve satisfaction.
Myntra should shift from discount-driven to value-driven growth by focusing on product quality and premium expansion for long-term profitability.
